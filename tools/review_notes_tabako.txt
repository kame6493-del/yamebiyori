2. Purpose and audience
Yamebiyori Tabako (やめ日和 たばこ, App Store name "やめ日和 禁煙カウンター・卒煙記録") is a self-tracking app for adults in Japan who want to quit smoking. It counts the time since the user quit (this streak, total, and longest), shows the money saved, and helps when a craving comes with a 5-minute timer, the user's own reason and coping options. If the user smokes, the app records it without blame: only the current streak restarts, and the total time and money saved are kept. The app is intended for users aged 20 and over (the legal smoking age in Japan). It is a recording tool and does not give medical advice or diagnosis; this is stated in the app.

3. How to use (no login or account is needed)
- Launch the app. On the first screen choose when you quit ("いまから" now, or "前から" a past date and time), enter the price of a pack, cigarettes per day and cigarettes per pack, optionally write a reason, and tap "はじめる".
- Home (きょう): this streak, total, money saved, longest streak and the next milestone. "吸いたくなった" opens the craving screen (5-minute timer, strength 1-5, situation, coping options, a table of situations and alternative actions; finish with "乗り切れた" or "吸った"). "吸った日を記録する" records a smoking day (number and a note).
- 記録 (Records): monthly calendar, craving records, the most effective coping methods, milestones and history.
- 体のこと (Health): public health information quoted from Japan's Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare, including the table of the effects of quitting (from 20 minutes to 15 years). Periods that have passed since the last cigarette are marked; the app states that the mark means only that the period has passed, not that the body has changed.
- 設定 (Settings): milestone notifications and time, colors, backup export (file) and import (file or pasted text), delete all records, Full Version and "購入の復元" (restore purchase).
All basic features are free. There are no ads.

4. External services
- Apple In-App Purchase, managed through RevenueCat (purchase validation and restore). RevenueCat receives only an anonymous app user ID and the App Store transaction information; no name, email address or record content.
- No other servers, no login service, no analytics, no ads, no AI services. Records are stored only on the device. Milestone notifications are local notifications scheduled on the device. Images and backups are passed to the system share sheet only when the user taps the button.

5. Regional differences
The app is intended for Japan. The content is in Japanese and the app works the same in every region.

6. Third-party material
The health information (the "体のこと" tab and the craving screen) is quoted word for word (without modification) from public pages of Japan's Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare (MHLW), with the title, site name, author, update date and URL shown in the app:
- e-health net (e-ヘルスネット, 厚生労働省 健康づくりサポートネット) articles t-08-001 (禁煙の効果), t-06-003 and t-06-002: https://kennet.mhlw.go.jp/information/information/tobacco/ . Its terms of use allow free use when the content is unmodified and the source is stated: https://kennet.mhlw.go.jp/ ("コンテンツの利用について"). They are based on the Public Data License (Version 1.0) of the Government of Japan, which allows commercial use: https://www.digital.go.jp/resources/open_data/public_data_license_v1.0
The app also states that the content was not provided by MHLW for this app; the app is not affiliated with or endorsed by MHLW. All other text and design are our own work. Open-source libraries: Capacitor and its plugins, @revenuecat/purchases-capacitor, React.

7. In-App Purchase
One non-consumable product, "完全版" (Full Version, product ID yamebiyori_tabako_full), JPY 600, one-time purchase (no subscription). It unlocks charts by time of day, weekday and money saved; milestone images and lock-screen wallpaper images; 5 color themes; and tracking several habits at once (tobacco, alcohol or others).
How to reach it: Home > tap the "完全版 買い切り" card, or 記録 tab > "完全版でひらく" on the locked charts (also the "画像(完全版)" and "待ち受け画像をつくる(完全版)" buttons), or 設定 > 完全版 > "完全版について", or tap a locked color. The purchase screen shows the free / full comparison table and the buy button. "以前に買った方はこちら(購入の復元)" (restore) is on the purchase screen, and "購入の復元" is also in Settings.
